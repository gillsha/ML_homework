1. Course Context
This is LLM4Rec, an AI-Assisted Recommender Systems course at HSE University. You will learn 
both classical RecSys algorithms (matrix factorizaƟon, content-based ﬁltering, collaboraƟve 
ﬁltering, PageRank, two-tower retrieval) and modern LLM-based approaches (LLM4Rec, 
conversaƟonal RecSys, agenƟc RecSys).
AI use is free to use. The rubric measures exactly two things about you:
① Understanding — c1
   Did you understand the problem? Did you find valid references?
② AI Management — c2
   Did you direct AI to produce a working solution?
   Did you verify AI's work yourself?
   Is your reasoning accurate (not just plausible)?
The Top-Down 5-Loop is the operaƟonal form of these two criteria:
① BUILD    → run a working baseline (AI may scaffold this)
② ASK WHY  → question assumptions, list alternatives (PERSONAL — your 
reasoning must be accurate)
③ TEST     → verify AI outputs against theory/data/code (PERSONAL — hand-
compute at least one result)
④ EXPLAIN  → state the result in YOUR OWN WORDS (PERSONAL — not AI's summary)
⑤ IMPROVE  → iterate on one variable, repeat
A student who lets AI produce working code without verifying the reasoning path will oŌen 
trigger Gate 0 — because AI output frequently contains subtly wrong root causes, fabricated 
mechanisms, or self-contradictory reasoning that look plausible but fail on veriﬁcaƟon.
See §8 for the exact rubric criteria.

7. The Top-Down 5-Loop — Pedagogical Core
This course is built around a speciﬁc learning philosophy. Read this secƟon carefully; it explains 
why the homework is structured the way it is.
7.1 The 5 steps
① BUILD    → run a working baseline (you can use AI to scaffold this)
② ASK WHY  → question assumptions, list alternatives (PERSONAL)
③ TEST     → verify AI outputs against theory/data/code (PERSONAL)
④ EXPLAIN  → state the result in YOUR OWN WORDS (PERSONAL)
⑤ IMPROVE  → iterate on one variable, repeat
7.2 The trap we are avoiding
It is tempƟng to:
 Prompt AI: "Build me a RecSys with X"
 AI returns working code + explanaƟon
 Submit the AI's output
This is the AI-delegaƟon trap. Students who do this:
 Score 0 on §5 Discussion (it reads like AI output)
 Score 0 on §6 AI Usage Disclosure (claim veriﬁed what wasn't)
 OŌen trigger Gate 0 (claims unveriﬁed)
 Do not actually learn the material
7.3 What we expect instead
 Prompt AI to scaﬀold (step ①)
 Stop and ask: "Is this the right approach? What are the alternaƟves? Why this and not 
that?" (step ②)
 Manually verify at least one number, one derivaƟon, one assumpƟon (step ③)
 Write the discussion in your own voice — not AI's summary (step ④)
 IdenƟfy what to do diﬀerently next based on what you learned (step ⑤)
Students who engage with all 5 steps score 2/2. Students who only do step ① score 0–1/2.
